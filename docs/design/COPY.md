# PF Health Copy Doctrine

## Voice

Calm, direct, respectful, and specific. Explain the member's record before naming administrative terminology. Be useful without sounding certain beyond the evidence.

## Core rules

- Use “your sample record” in demo-facing copy where synthetic context could be forgotten.
- Say what is known, what it may affect, and what remains uncertain.
- Use “may need to” for actor guidance unless the source makes the requirement explicit for the named scenario.
- Name EPFO only when describing the official source or a supported process.
- Avoid blame, fear, urgency, legal advice, and success guarantees.
- Never call deterministic output “AI detection.”
- Never imply a simulation was submitted externally.

## Preferred replacements

| Avoid | Use |
| --- | --- |
| DOE missing in service history | Your previous employment is missing exit information. |
| Claim eligible | No known blockers were detected by the checks supported in this prototype. |
| Employer fault | Your previous employer may need to update or confirm this record. |
| AI detected | PF Health found |
| Invalid PF account | We could not confirm this record. |
| Fix now | Review what to do next |
| Submit correction | Apply simulated correction |
| All clear | 5 of 5 supported checks look healthy |
| Guaranteed to work | This does not guarantee a transfer or claim outcome. |

## Recurring headings

- What needs attention
- Why this matters
- Who needs to act
- What to do next
- Why we're saying this
- What changed
- Activity timeline

## Approved hero-case copy

### Summary

**4 of 5 checks look healthy**

One item in Ravi's sample record needs attention before the supported online-transfer scenario.

### Issue title

**Your previous employment is missing exit information.**

### Impact

EPFO's published transfer guidance says the previous employment's exit date must be entered for an online PF transfer. This prototype does not determine whether every transfer or claim will fail.

### Owner

**Who needs to act:** Ravi may be able to use the supported member update path in the applicable scenario. If the record cannot be updated that way, the previous employer or EPFO review may be needed.

The application must derive the displayed owner/path from the structured resolution action, not hard-code this paragraph independently.

### Simulation notice

**This changes only Ravi's sample record. Nothing will be sent to EPFO or an employer.**

### Healthy state

**5 of 5 supported checks look healthy**

No known blockers were detected by the checks supported in this prototype.

## Error and unknown patterns

- Loading: **Checking Ravi's sample record…**
- Revalidation: **Rechecking the updated sample record…**
- Load error: **We couldn't load the sample record. Reset it and try again.**
- Unknown: **We could not confirm this check from the available sample data.**
- AI fallback: do not mention model failure; display approved deterministic copy. A non-blocking notice may say **Using standard explanation** if useful.

## Drafting constraints

A correction request identifies the synthetic member, employment record, missing field, requested review/update, and attached synthetic evidence if any. It must not contain threats, legal conclusions, fabricated reference numbers, or statements that the sender contacted EPFO when they did not.
